# Activity 02: Computer Vision Research

## Overview

The purpose of this activity was to research a real-world application of computer vision and understand how the technology works. For this assignment, I chose **facial recognition** because it is one of the most common applications of computer vision and artificial intelligence.

The assignment included researching the technology, explaining how it works, discussing its advantages and challenges, and reflecting on its possible future impact on society.

---

## Objectives

- Research a real-world computer vision application.
- Understand how computer vision analyzes images.
- Explain the technologies used in facial recognition.
- Discuss the benefits and limitations of the technology.
- Reflect on future developments and social impact.

---

## Project Description

This research explains the process of facial recognition, starting with face detection, image normalization, feature extraction, and matching a face against a database. The paper also describes how computer vision and deep learning models work together to recognize a person's identity.

In addition, the research discusses practical applications of facial recognition, including smartphone security, access control, and law enforcement. It also addresses concerns related to privacy, data security, and ethical use of the technology.

---

## Technologies Discussed

- Computer Vision
- Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)
- Face Embeddings (Faceprints)
- Image Processing

---

## Key Takeaways

This activity helped me understand how facial recognition systems analyze images and convert facial features into mathematical representations that can be compared with stored data. It also showed me that, although this technology provides many benefits, it must be used responsibly because of the ethical and privacy concerns involved.
